Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

A note from me

Hi! I'm a parent, and I made Flashy for my son so he could practice math and typing on his own. Since it's for kids, I kept it simple on purpose: there are no online accounts, no ads, and nothing about your child gets sent to me. I've read through this page myself to make sure it matches how Flashy really works.

In short

  • There are no online accounts. Your child just types a name, with no email or password.
  • Progress is saved in your web browser on your device, not sent to me.
  • No ads, no tracking, and I don't sell or share anything about your child.
  • There's no chat or messaging between students.

What Flashy saves

To remember each student, Flashy saves this only in the browser on your device (using the browser's built-in storage):

  • The name and grade typed in on the sign-in screen (a first name or nickname is all that's needed)
  • Avatar, frame and name color choices, and gems
  • Progress: courses, scores, typing lessons, typing speed, badges, streaks and daily chests
  • Settings like sound, music and the easier-to-read font

I can't see any of this. It never leaves your device unless you choose to save a backup file.

Flashy also keeps a copy of its own pages on your device so it can open without internet. That's just the app itself, not anything about your child.

Backup files

In Progress → Settings you can save a student's progress to a file and load it on another device. That file is saved where you choose, and only you control it. Flashy doesn't upload it anywhere.

Other services Flashy uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides Flashy's fonts (including the easier-to-read font, if it's turned on). When the page loads a font, your browser asks Google for it, so Google may see your IP address. Flashy sends Google no student information. See Google Fonts and privacy.

Flashy doesn't use analytics, ad networks, trackers or cookies.

Children's privacy

Flashy is made for kids. It doesn't collect or send any personal information to me, so there's no online account for a child to create and nothing for me to store. You can see all of your child's progress in the Progress tab, including the For parents section at the bottom.

Deleting information

  • To delete one student: on the sign-in screen, tap I have an account!, then the ⋯ button next to the student, then Delete.
  • To delete everything: clear this website's data in your browser's settings.

Questions?

If you have a question about privacy, or anything else about Flashy, email me at asherndahl@gmail.com. I read every message.

Changes

If anything about how Flashy handles information changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
