Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts online. Flashy doesn't have sign-ups, emails or passwords.
  • Progress stays on your device. It's saved in your web browser, not sent to us.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Ever.
  • No chat or messaging between players.

What Flashy saves

To remember each player, Flashy saves this only in the browser on your device (using the browser's built-in storage):

  • The name and grade typed in on the sign-in screen (a first name or nickname is all that's needed)
  • Avatar, frame and name color choices, and gems
  • Progress: courses, scores, typing lessons, typing speed, badges, streaks and daily chests
  • Settings like sound, music and the easier-to-read font

We (the people who make Flashy) can't see any of this. It never leaves your device unless you choose to save a backup file.

Backup files

In Progress → Settings you can save a player's progress to a file and load it on another device. That file is saved where you choose, and only you control it. Flashy doesn't upload it anywhere.

Other services Flashy uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides Flashy's fonts. When the page loads a font, your browser asks Google for it, so Google may see your IP address. Flashy sends Google no player information.

Flashy has no analytics, ad networks, trackers or cookies of its own.

Children's privacy

Flashy is made for kids. Because Flashy doesn't collect or send any personal information to us, there's no account for a child to create online and nothing for us to store. Parents can see all of a child's progress in the Progress tab, including the For parents section.

Deleting information

  • To delete one player: on the sign-in screen, tap I have an account!, then the ⋯ button next to the player, then Delete.
  • To delete everything: clear this website's data in your browser's settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
