Flashy for parents

Fast math practice and typing lessons for Kindergarten through 8th grade, made so kids can use it on their own.

Why I made Flashy

I'm a parent, and I made Flashy for my son. I wanted one place where he could practice math facts and learn to type without me sitting next to him the whole time, that I'd also feel good about him using. I've gone over everything on this page myself, and I'm still adding to Flashy and fixing things as we use it.

Getting started

  1. Your child types a first name or nickname, picks their grade and an avatar.
  2. In the Play tab they pick a math subject and tap Start course, or open Typing lessons.
  3. New to typing? Start with the placement test on the Typing lessons page. It finds the right first lesson.
  4. Short daily practice works best: one math course and one typing lesson takes about 10 minutes.

Using Flashy independently

  • Math is set to your child's grade automatically, so there's nothing to set up.
  • Each course ends with a second chance at missed questions, and missed questions come back later as review questions.
  • Typing lessons unlock one at a time once your child reaches 90% accuracy, so they always know what to do next.
  • The on-screen keyboard lights up the next key and shows which finger to use.
  • Gems are earned (never bought) for practicing, with the most for perfect work, plus badges and daily logins. They're spent on avatars and colors.

Checking progress

Open the Progress tab and scroll to For parents to see:

  • Accuracy in each math subject, and which one needs the most practice
  • How many questions were answered each day this week
  • Typing lessons passed, typing speed (words per minute) and accuracy over time

Math: what's covered

Two ways to practice: Type the answer (simpler numbers, like 34 + 7) and Multiple choice (the full range for the grade).

Typing: lesson order

  1. Home row: left hand (A S D F)
  2. Home row: right hand (J K L ;)
  3. The whole home row
  4. G and H
  5. E and I
  6. R and U
  7. T and Y
  8. W and O
  9. Q and P
  10. V and M
  11. C and comma
  12. X and period
  13. Z, B and N
  14. Capital letters (Shift)
  15. Numbers 1 to 5
  16. Numbers 6 to 0
  17. Question and exclamation marks

After the lessons, the typing tests (Easy, Medium, Hard) build speed with real sentences.

Saving progress and switching devices

Progress is saved on the device your child uses. To move it to another device, open Progress → Settings → Back up to save a file, then on the other device choose Restore (or Restore from a backup file on the sign-in screen).

Tip: back up now and then, so progress isn't lost if the browser's data is cleared.

Safety and privacy

No online accounts, no ads, no chat and no tracking. Progress never leaves your device. Read the full privacy page.
